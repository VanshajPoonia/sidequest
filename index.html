Who's going

The missions are written by Gemma 3 (Google's open-weight model) running in this tab. The first quest downloads it, 860 MB, once; best on wifi, before you head out. Nothing you choose is sent to an AI service.

Your quest

    Once written, this quest is saved on your phone and opens with no signal. Prove a stop with a photo and a vision model checks it on this phone (89 MB, once). Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

    Team event

    Several teams walk this quest at once, prove each stop with a photo, and race on a live leaderboard. You get a code to share and a dashboard.

    Time limit

    No account, no server, no API key. Stops are never chosen by the model: code picks them from the map and checks the route fits your time. Gemma only writes, and anything it writes that is not backed by the map gets sent back. Source
